Selected work

From pixels to decisions

PhD · PNRR TECH4YOU · 2023 to 2026

Vegetation vigor monitoring in Mediterranean crops

Question
How can multi-sensor imagery track crop vigor reliably across different farms and scales?
Data
Sentinel-2, PlanetScope SuperDove and UAV imagery across four case studies
Work
Reproducible pipelines that turn raw imagery into spectral indices and time series, in Python (Rasterio, GeoPandas, GDAL, xarray), packaged with Docker and Git and run on HPC
Role
Project lead: planning, analysis, reporting to the funding programme and dissemination
UM6P · 2020 to 2021

Four decades of land-use change

Data
Landsat archive, 1984 to 2020
Method
Decision-tree classification, first in ENVI, then rebuilt in Python and ArcGIS

Cropping shifted toward more profitable but more water-intensive crops, raising water consumption in a semi-arid region.
